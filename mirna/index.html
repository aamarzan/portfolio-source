About this tool

This tool predicts miRNA binding affinity to RNA targets and estimates competitive effects of optional competitor molecules. It fuses sequence features with optional 3D structure-derived features, and is trained on curated datasets with experimental binding information.


How it works

  • Input your miRNA(s) as multi‑FASTA, plus a single target sequence (FASTA or raw).
  • Optionally provide a competitor sequence (FASTA or raw) and 3D structure files (PDB/mmCIF).
  • Advanced options include auto‑trimming long miRNAs to a mature‑like window and lossy AA→NT back‑translation where allowed.
  • The backend validates inputs, encodes sequences and structures, and performs RAM‑safe batch model inference.

Results interpretation

  • Baseline affinity: predicted binding strength without competitor.
  • Affinity with competitor: predicted binding strength considering the competitor.
  • Competitive effect: difference between baseline and competitor conditions.
  • For best results, ensure that miRNA FASTA headers match any provided miRNA 3D filenames (e.g., >hsa-let-7a-5p → hsa-let-7a-5p.pdb).

    • Points to be noted

    • Predictions are based on training data and may not generalize to all miRNA-target pairs.
    • 3D structure files must correspond to the provided sequences; mismatches will be rejected.
    • Back-translation of amino acid sequences to nucleotide is lossy and may introduce inaccuracies.
    • Server resource limits may restrict input sizes and batch processing capabilities.
